06 / HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS

Three measures.
Three different questions.

Power, water and compute are related, but none can stand in for the others. For local impacts, use power and water. Compute is a scale measure only.

01
ϟ

How much power?

energy (MWh) = MW × hours × utilization

Stated capacity is the most a campus can draw. Average draw is lower. Meta's 2024 fleet drew about half of the capacity we track for it. PUE is facility energy ÷ IT energy, so a PUE of 1.10 adds 10% for cooling and power losses.

POWER · NAMEPLATE IS NOT DRAW
02
◒

How much water?

WUE = water litres ÷ IT kWh

Withdrawal is the water a campus takes. Consumption is the water it does not return, mostly by evaporation. They are not interchangeable. Outside Google, our site water figures are a fleet average applied to the site, not a meter reading.

WATER · CHECK THE BASIS
03

How much compute?

H100e = compute ÷ 1.979 PFLOPS

H100-equivalents compare sites with different chips. The value comes from announced GPU counts, third-party estimates, peak FLOPS or power. 68% of the total comes from power, so it adds no new information about resource use.
